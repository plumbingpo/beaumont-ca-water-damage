Few emergencies in a building are as hazardous as sewage backing up into it. Raw sewage contains dangerous bacteria, viruses, parasites and other pathogens, including E. coli, hepatitis, salmonella and giardia, that pose serious health risks to anyone who touches contaminated materials. Beaumont Restoration Co provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Beaumont, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

The industry classifies sewage as black water, or Category 3, which is the most dangerous and most contaminated type of water damage there is. This classification requires the most stringent cleanup protocols, including full personal protective equipment, containment barriers, removal of all porous materials that contacted sewage and thorough disinfection of every remaining surface. We work to the IICRC S500 standard that governs Category 3 losses, with the goal of returning your property to a condition safe to occupy.

The cleanup process involves removing all standing sewage, extracting contaminated water from structural materials, removing and properly disposing of every porous material that contacted sewage (carpet, padding, drywall, insulation), thoroughly disinfecting hard surfaces, applying antimicrobial treatments and completing structural drying to prevent secondary mold growth. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

What a Professional Brings to Sewage Cleanup

No homeowner should try to clean up sewage personally. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. Exposure to sewage pathogens can cause serious illness, including gastrointestinal disease, hepatitis, respiratory infections and skin infections.

Disposal matters as well, since everything the sewage contaminated has to be handled as biohazard waste. Simply placing sewage-contaminated drywall, carpet and insulation in your regular trash is both illegal and hazardous to sanitation workers and the community. Certified restoration companies maintain proper waste disposal protocols and relationships with licensed biohazard waste disposal facilities.

Signs You Need Sewage Cleanup in Beaumont

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Slow drains throughout the house that plunging does not fix
  • Toilet water with waste in it soaking into carpet or running across the floor
  • Toilets or drains that gurgle whenever you run water somewhere else
  • Sewage or dark water backing up through floor drains, toilets or tubs
  • Unexplained illness or gastrointestinal symptoms among household members
  • Damp patches on a wall or floor that also smell foul
  • A cleanout that weeps sewage, or wet foul patches in the lawn along the sewer path

Sewage Cleanup Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Install a backwater valve on your sewer connection to prevent backups from the municipal system
  • Hair and other debris are easy to stop with simple strainers on tub, shower and sink drains
  • Keep cooking oil, grease and food scraps out of the drain, where they build into clogs
  • Address slow drains promptly, before they become complete blockages
  • Have your sewer line inspected with a camera every 2 to 3 years to catch potential problems
  • Stormwater from a sump or downspout should never drain into the sewer, since the extra volume invites a backup
